# Customer Retention Rate (CRR) — 2024

## Quarterly Data
- Q1: 69.02%
- Q2: 68.11%
- Q3: 75.98%
- Q4: 75.22%

**Average (2024):** 72.08%  
**Industry Target:** 85%

---

## Key Findings
- The **average CRR of 72.08%** is significantly below the **industry benchmark of 85%**.  
- **Q1 and Q2** show the weakest performance (<70%), pointing to poor early-year retention practices.  
- **Q3 (75.98%)** marks the strongest quarter, suggesting positive outcomes from specific campaigns or product improvements.  
- **Q4 (75.22%)** maintained the improvement but did not close the retention gap.  

---

## Business Implications
- The company is **~13 percentage points behind industry peers**, indicating higher churn and reduced customer lifetime value.  
- **Acquisition costs** may not be fully justified if retention remains low.  
- The improvement in H2 demonstrates that targeted strategies can work when scaled consistently.  

---

## Recommendations
**Primary Recommendation:** *Strengthen customer success and lifecycle engagement.*  
- Improve **onboarding programs** (guided setups, proactive early support).  
- Use **predictive churn analytics** to identify at-risk customers.  
- Incentivize loyalty with **contracts, rewards, and referral programs**.  

**Other tactical moves:**  
- Replicate **Q3 best practices** across other quarters.  
- Run **NPS & satisfaction surveys** to pinpoint churn causes.  
- Enhance **product stickiness** with integrations, add-ons, and bundled offerings.  
- Offer **enterprise SLAs** to build trust with high-value customers.
